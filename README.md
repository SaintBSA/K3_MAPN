# K3 Safety Inspection & Equipment Management System

An interactive web-based Occupational Health, Safety, and Environment (K3/OHSE) management platform designed to streamline safety reporting, asset tracking, and compliance monitoring for industrial environments such as PT Multi Aneka Pangan Nusantara. Built to complement mobile inspection workflows, this platform provides administrators and safety officers with a centralized dashboard to track safety equipment, process incoming hazard reports, and manage QR-based asset registries in real time.

---

## Key Features

- **Centralized K3 Asset Dashboard:** Monitor the status, location, and maintenance schedules of all site safety equipment (e.g., fire extinguishers, eye wash stations, and PPE inventory).
- **Hazard & Incident Reporting Hub:** Review incoming reports submitted by field inspectors, assign maintenance tasks, and track resolution statuses.
- **QR Code Management & Generation:** Generate and download unique QR code identifiers for physical equipment to enable instant field scanning and asset lookup.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Secure admin panel ensuring role-specific permissions for safety managers, field inspectors, and maintenance teams.
- **Inspection Analytics & Exporting:** View inspection trends, identify recurring equipment failures, and export safety compliance reports for auditing.
